# Credit-steals & propaganda misattributions — a sourced briefing

*For outreach. Each claim carries its receipt. Even-handed: where a real TVK action
exists, we say so. Feed to NotebookLM for an explainer.*

## What this is
Two patterns the dashboard tracks: (1) the **TVK government** claiming credit for
DMK-era schemes; (2) **fan/page propaganda cards** misattributing DMK or Central actions
to CM Vijay. Below are the verified flagship cases.

---

## Case 1 — "CM Vijay rejects the ₹20,000 cr Adani smart-meter project" → FALSE
- **The Adani deal was killed by the DMK, not Vijay.** The Stalin/DMK government
  **cancelled the Adani Energy Solutions smart-meter tender on 27 December 2024** because
  the price was too high (Adani was lowest bidder for ~82 lakh meters) — **16+ months
  before Vijay took office.**
- **Mar/Nov 2025:** DMK re-tendered a larger ~₹20,000 cr project (~3.04 cr meters). **No
  Adani vendor from here on.**
- **Early June 2026:** the TVK government **shelved the whole (post-Adani, vendor-less)
  project**, citing EB irregularities + fiscal strain. The **BJP criticised** it as risking
  ~₹5,000 cr in central RDSS grants.
- **Verdict:** the viral card **welds DMK's 2024 Adani cancellation onto TVK's 2026
  project-shelving** to credit Vijay with "rejecting Adani." A real TVK shelving exists —
  so it's not wholly fabricated — but the **"rejects Adani" claim is false.**
- *Sources: Business Today, Deccan Herald, Business Standard (Dec 2024); DT Next (Jun 2026).*

## Case 2 — Farmer crop-loan waiver: a U-turn under protest
- **Manifesto promise:** full waiver for farmers up to 5 acres.
- **25 May 2026:** TVK delivered a **diluted, slabbed** scheme (100% only up to ₹50,000;
  ~₹2,044 cr; 14.22 lakh farmers), justified by **RBI's Nov-2025 debt-waiver guidelines**.
- **Protests** (Erode 1 Jun, Coimbatore, Perambalur).
- **16 Jun 2026:** CM Vijay **expanded it to 100% up to ₹75,000** (cooperative banks).
- **Verdict:** the expansion genuinely **helps more farmers** (credit it) — but it's a
  **U-turn** that undercuts (a) the "RBI rules cap us" justification and (b) the CM's
  10-May claim that DMK "emptied the treasury." *(A circulating "₹6,000 cr" cost and a
  minister's "didn't see the protesters" remark are UNVERIFIED — we don't use them.)*
- *Sources: @CMOTamilnadu (16 Jun); ETV Bharat (25 May); Business Today (10 May); The News Minute (protests).*

## Case 3 — "Yogi Babu donated ₹1 crore to Amma Unavagam" → FABRICATED
- No credible source. Markers of a fake card: a misspelled fan handle, "BREAKING NEWS"
  template, Instagram-follow bait, and the incoherence of crediting **TVK** with **Amma
  Unavagam** (an AIADMK scheme). Logged as manufactured propaganda.

---

## How to read these honestly
- A **credit-steal** = presenting someone else's work as your own. A **misattribution** =
  a card crediting Vijay for what DMK or the Centre did.
- We always state the **real TVK action** when one exists (e.g. the genuine smart-meter
  shelving, the genuine farmer-waiver expansion) — the dishonesty is in the *attribution*
  or the *framing*, not always the underlying event.
