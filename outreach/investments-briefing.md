# Tamil Nadu investment scorecard — a sourced briefing

*For outreach. Every figure is source-linked on the dashboard. Even-handed: it
credits the wins and flags the fine print. Feed to NotebookLM for an explainer.*

## The honest headline
Tamil Nadu attracted **huge investment commitments** under the DMK government — but a
**commitment (MoU) is not money delivered.** Of ~₹2.98 lakh crore in flagship MoUs, only
about **9% is operational on the ground** so far. Credit the pipeline; track the delivery.

## The numbers (sourced)
- **GIM 2024 (Jan 2024):** Tamil Nadu signed **₹6.64 lakh crore across 631 MoUs**. Our
  scorecard tracks the **flagship subset (~₹2.98 lakh cr)** by value.
- **Built vs MoU (the key split):**
  - **Operational (live):** ~₹27,000 cr (~9%) — e.g. Tata Electronics, Ola, Hyundai, Pegatron.
  - **Under construction:** ~₹25,000 cr — e.g. VinFast (inaugurated Aug 2025), L&T, Royal Enfield.
  - **MoU only (signed, not built):** ~₹2.3 lakh cr — e.g. Tata Power renewables (₹70,800 cr),
    Adani (₹42,768 cr), Sembcorp green hydrogen (₹36,238 cr).
  - **At risk:** Mazagon Dock (₹15,000 cr) — reportedly drifting to Andhra Pradesh.
- The govt's claimed **"72% MoU-to-ground conversion"** is a self-report, not an audited figure.

## The fine print that keeps it honest
- **An MoU is intent, not delivered money.** "₹3 lakh crore invested" reads as money in the
  ground; most of it is signed-but-unbuilt.
- **Watch the big "committed" items** — Tata Power's ₹70,800 cr is a broad ~10 GW renewables
  MoU (not the small Tirunelveli factory it's often confused with).
- **Don't credit Central PSU projects to the state** — e.g. the CPCL/IOCL Cauvery refinery is
  a central project, not a state MoU (we removed it for that reason).
- **Every commitment on the dashboard is source-linked** — that's the standard.

## The fair steelman
The pipeline is real and large, conversion is reportedly above the national average, and
genuinely grounded wins exist (VinFast, the operational electronics plants). The honest
ask isn't "it's all fake" — it's **"show the ground delivery, don't read MoUs as money."**
