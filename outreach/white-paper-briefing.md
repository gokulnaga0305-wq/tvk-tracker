# TVK white paper on TN finances — a sourced fact-check briefing

*For outreach. Even-handed: it concedes what's true and debunks the spin. Feed
to NotebookLM for an audio/video explainer.*

## What this is
On **16 June 2026** the TVK government (FM N. Marie Wilson) released a **white paper**
on Tamil Nadu's finances, alleging the DMK (2021–26) left the state in fiscal crisis —
fulfilling CM Vijay's 10-May claim that DMK "emptied the treasury."

## One-line verdict
**Political, not a neutral audit.** Some concerns are real — but the headline framing
(scary absolute debt, an inflated debt ratio compared unfairly to other states,
overstated "record" deficits, and "DMK emptied the treasury") is misleading.

## The honest comparison (judge debt by GSDP, not the raw number)
- **AIADMK (left 2021):** ~₹5 lakh cr debt on ₹17 lakh cr GSDP = **29%**.
- **DMK (2025-26):** ~₹10 lakh cr debt on ₹36 lakh cr GSDP = **28%**.
- The economy **doubled**, so the debt ratio actually **fell (29% → 28%)** and stays
  **within the ~30% statutory limit.**
- Simple analogy: who owes "less" — someone earning ₹50,000 with a ₹5,000 loan, or
  earning ₹1,00,000 with a ₹7,000 loan? You judge debt against income — i.e. GSDP.

## TN vs other big states (debt as % of GSDP)
Punjab ~46% · West Bengal ~37% · Kerala ~36% · Rajasthan ~35% · Andhra ~33% ·
all-states avg ~28% · **Tamil Nadu ~26%.** TN is among the **better-managed** large states.

## What's FAIR in the white paper (concede this)
- **Interest payments are high** — ~₹67,000 cr ≈ **22% of revenue receipts** (~2× the
  prudential norm, now above annual capex). *(But it already hit 21% in 2020-21, under AIADMK.)*
- TN runs a **persistent revenue deficit** (borrows partly for day-to-day spend) — a real weak spot.
- Absolute debt did **roughly double** to ~₹10 lakh cr (in rupees).
- Government **guarantees + TANGEDCO liabilities** are large and growing.

## What's MISLEADING (the spin)
- **Debt-to-GSDP "28.3%"** — the standard PRS figure is **~26% and declining**, within the
  limit. The 28.3% folds in off-budget liabilities, then compares to other states' *narrow*
  numbers. Apples-to-oranges.
- **Deficits "highest ever" (3.77% / 2.22%)** — PRS puts fiscal deficit **at the 3% FRBM
  target** and revenue deficit at **1.2% and narrowing.** Magnitudes inflated; improving trend hidden.
- **"DMK emptied the treasury"** — no audited basis (accounts are CAG-audited). Much debt was
  **inherited**: 20% line crossed under AIADMK (2016-17); UDAY power-debt signed by AIADMK
  (Jan 2017); steepest jump in COVID 2020-21.
- **"Corruption in tax departments"** — asserted with zero evidence.
- Context omitted: the **Centre cut TN's share** of central taxes and delayed dues — on-time
  funds would have meant less borrowing.

## DMK rebuttal
Ex-FM **Thangam Thennarasu**: "an empty paper, not a white paper." Spokesperson
**A. Saravanan**: "judge by the GSDP ratio — it's within Finance Commission limits" (factually sound).

## Bottom line
TN is a rich, fast-growing economy (~9% of India's GDP) that is fiscally
middling-to-stretched. The white paper's **facts** point to real issues; its **framing** is
a political document, not a neutral audit.
