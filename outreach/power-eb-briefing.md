# Tamil Nadu electricity (EB) bills under DMK (2021–2026) — a sourced briefing

*For outreach. Every figure is sourced; the framing is even-handed so it holds
up to scrutiny. Feed to NotebookLM for an audio/video explainer.*

## The claim being checked
"DMK is the sole reason your EB bill goes up every year."

## One-line verdict
**Overstated.** The yearly rise is an **inflation-linked TNERC true-up**, not a
fresh DMK decision each year — it corrects an 8-year tariff freeze, and ordinary
households are largely **shielded** by subsidy. DMK *did* build the automatic
mechanism (2022); it is *not* "DMK arbitrarily hikes households every year."

## The timeline (sourced)
- **No hike for the first ~16 months** of DMK (May 2021 → Sep 2022).
- **10 Sep 2022 — TNERC Tariff Order No. 7 of 2022:** the **first revision since 2014**
  (an 8-year freeze). Domestic energy charge ₹3 → **₹4.50/unit** (≤400 units), higher
  slabs up to ₹11. It also **built in an automatic annual CPI-linked revision** (capped
  6%, every 1 July through FY27).
- **1 Jul 2023:** +**2.18%** (automatic CPI revision).
- **1 Jul 2024:** +**4.83%** — domestic +₹0.20–0.50/unit; fixed charge ₹102 → ₹107/kW.
- **1 Jul 2025:** +**3.16%** — but **domestic, small traders, MSMEs, agriculture, huts
  shielded** via ~₹519.84 cr subsidy.

## Who really decides the annual hike?
- **TNERC**, the independent statutory regulator (Electricity Act 2003) — *not* the
  government directly. The govt doesn't pick the number each year.
- The **annual % is inflation-linked** (Consumer Price Index): 2.18%, 4.83%, 3.16%.
- It corrects a **2014–2022 freeze** — most of which was under the **AIADMK** government —
  that under-priced power and helped bankrupt TANGEDCO (~₹1.6 lakh cr debt).
- **The same mechanism now runs under TVK:** the **July-2026 hike fires under the DMK-era
  2022 order** — so "DMK chooses to hike every year" breaks the moment the next one happens
  under a different party.

## Who's shielded
- **100 free units** per two-month cycle for domestic consumers — retained throughout.
- **Free power for hut dwellers** — retained.
- From 2023 the govt increasingly **absorbed** the hikes for domestic & protected
  categories; **fully shielded in 2025**. The increases fall mainly on **commercial /
  industrial / HT** and high-consumption domestic users.

## The honest steelman
DMK *did* create the automatic-revision structure in 2022, and a chunk of the "growth"
in bills is price/tax revisions, not more usage. Fair to say: **DMK is the architect of
the annual mechanism.** Not fair: **"DMK alone raises your household bill every year"** —
that ignores TNERC, CPI indexing, the AIADMK-era freeze, and the domestic shield.
