## The accountability question

Both governments announced TASMAC closures. The test of a real, auditable closure
is: **is there a Government Order / official abstract + an itemised shop list the
public can verify?** DMK met that bar. TVK (so far) has not.

## DMK — 500 shops (2023): itemised, auditable ✅

Official TASMAC abstract, **"DETAILS OF SHOPS TO BE CLOSED"**, signed by the
Managing Director, dated **26.06.2023**. Lists shops to be closed **by individual
shop number, district by district**.

| Region      | Total shops (31.03.2023) | To be closed |
|-------------|--------------------------|--------------|
| Chennai     | 905                      | 138          |
| Coimbatore  | 933                      | 78           |
| Madurai     | 1,345                    | ~95          |
| Salem       | 1,001                    | 89           |
| Trichy      | 1,145                    | 100          |
| **Grand total** | **5,329**            | **500**      |

Example granularity — Chennai (N): shop nos. 101, 132, 138, 164, 182, 206, 215,
248, 255, 273, 3, 30, 104, 107, 143, 221, 245, 283, 286, 296 (20 shops).
→ Anyone can go to a listed shop number and confirm it is shut. **Auditable.**

## TVK — 717 shops (2026): announced, not itemised ❌

- **What exists:** CM order + a **DIPR (Directorate of Information & Public
  Relations) press release.**
- **Category split only:** 276 near temples / 186 near schools / 255 near bus stands.
- **Employees:** 3,474 affected, stated as redeployed.
- **What is missing:** No published shop-by-shop list (none found as of 2026-06-10).
- **Press framing:** The Federal — *"Vijay orders closure of 717 Tasmac outlets:
  Stern action or sheer optics?"*
  (https://thefederal.com/category/states/south/tamil-nadu/cm-vijay-orders-closure-717-tasmac-liquor-shops-242804)
- **Unverified ground claim (user):** private bars reportedly opened near the
  closed outlets. Not confirmed in news; needs photo/location evidence to document.

## Related — 67 quarries (May 2026): temporary, not permanent

- Officially **"67 units shut down temporarily, pending further examination and
  legal action"** — penalty proceedings initiated. (News Today, 2026-05-30)
- Virudhunagar's 23 also "temporarily suspended."
- **No official data** on permanent closures, lease cancellations, or reopenings.
- → The poster's "20+ closed" overstates a *reversible temporary suspension*.

## Honest verdict

The transparency-standard gap is **real and documented on both sides' own paper**:
DMK published an itemised, verifiable list; TVK released a press statement with only
a category split. This is not "DMK good / TVK bad" — it is a concrete, defensible
contrast: **one government let the public verify, the other asked for trust.**
